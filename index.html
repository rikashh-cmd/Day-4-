RED MACAW

Parrots, members of the order Psittaciformes, encompass over 360 species of vibrant, noisy birds. These primarily tropical birds have a chunky build, short necks and wings, and a distinctive, strong, hooked bill where the upper part extends over the lower part.
Parrot families include Psittacidae, containing parakeets, lovebirds, macaws, and more, and Cacatuidae, the cockatoos, native to Australia and New Guinea.
